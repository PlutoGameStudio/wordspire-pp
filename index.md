# Privacy Policy — WordSpire

**Last updated: 15 August 2026**

WordSpire is a vocabulary game for Android, published by Pluto Studio.

This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **it does not
collect any.**

## What we collect

Nothing.

WordSpire has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers and no analytics. It requests **no Android
permissions at all** — including no internet permission, which means the app is technically
incapable of sending anything anywhere. It does not collect your name, email address, location,
contacts, advertising identifier, or any other personal or device identifier.

## What is stored on your device

The app saves your progress locally so the game can continue where you left it. This data stays
in the app's private storage on your phone and is not readable by other apps:

- which words you have reviewed and when they are next due (the spaced-repetition schedule)
- your completed climbs — floor reached, topic, difficulty, accuracy and gold earned
- your card collection and the deck you composed
- your settings, daily-goal streak and chosen language course

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Android backup

If you have **Android Backup** switched on in your device settings, Android may copy the app's
saved data to your own Google Drive account so it can be restored when you set up a new phone.
This is a feature of the Android operating system, not of WordSpire. The backup belongs to your
Google account, is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and the developer of WordSpire
has no access to it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

## Speech

WordSpire can read a sentence aloud when you press the speaker button on a prompt. It never
speaks on its own. Speech is produced by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your
device, which is separate software governed by its own privacy policy — on most phones this is
Google's, and it can typically speak the languages in this app without a network connection.
WordSpire does not record audio and does not request microphone access.

## Children

WordSpire collects no data from anyone, children included. There is nothing to delete, because
nothing is gathered in the first place.

## Advertising and purchases

The current version of WordSpire contains **no advertising, no analytics and no in-app
purchases**.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds anything that handles data — for example Google Play Games
sign-in and leaderboards, or advertising — this policy will be updated to describe it before that
version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
